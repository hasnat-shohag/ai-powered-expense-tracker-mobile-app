## Direction contract

THESIS: The home screen is the category-standard expense tracker, executed impeccably — the number first, the detail beneath. It refuses the busy dashboard and the novelty metaphor: no invented chrome, no ornament, calm and correct is the whole point.

OWN-WORLD: White #FFFFFF ground, near-black #111827 ink, grey #6B7280 secondary, hairline #E5E7EB. One blue #2563EB for action only. Four flat category tints — amber #B4741B, blue #2C5FA8, green #2F7A3E, purple #8A3E8A — over pale fills. Roboto + Noto Sans Bengali. Soft white cards, 12px corners, faint shadow. Amber #FDF4E3 pair for offline/pending.

STORY: The user opens the app, reads the month total and its category split at a glance, scans recent expenses, and taps one blue pill to add another. Capture is never more than one tap away; the LLM parse is a draft the user confirms.
